# Wild Generation Seeds

Anti-obvious prompts for the 25% wild budget. Each seed forces generation into territory no LLM would reach unprompted. Move to "Used" after generating.

## Rules
- NEVER generate from a seed that sounds like a popular TED talk, Reddit TIL, or listicle topic
- The test: would this make someone say "I had no idea this existed" — not "oh yeah I've heard of that"
- Combine two unrelated seeds for maximum obscurity
